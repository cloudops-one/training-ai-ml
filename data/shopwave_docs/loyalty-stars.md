# ShopWave Stars loyalty programme

ShopWave Stars is free to join for every customer with an account.

## Earning

- You earn **1 Star for every £1** you spend (delivery charges and gift wrap do not count).
- In **November**, every purchase earns **double Stars**.
- Wholesale orders do not earn Stars.

## Spending

- **100 Stars = £5 off** your next order. You can redeem Stars in blocks of 100.
- Stars can be combined with a promo code.

## Expiry

Stars **expire after 12 months without a purchase**. Any purchase resets the 12-month clock for all your Stars.
