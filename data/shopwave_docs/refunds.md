# Refunds

## When will I get my money back?

We refund within **5 working days** of receiving your return at our warehouse. Your bank may take another 3 to 5 working days to show the money.

## How are refunds paid?

- Refunds go back to the **original payment method**.
- If you paid with a **gift voucher**, that part of the refund is issued as a new gift voucher.

## Delivery charges

- The original delivery charge is refunded only if you return the **whole order**, or if the item was **faulty or damaged**.
- Express delivery upgrades are never refunded unless the parcel arrived late.

## Partial refunds

If an item comes back used or damaged by the customer, we may refund part of its price. We email you a photo and the amount before we do this.
