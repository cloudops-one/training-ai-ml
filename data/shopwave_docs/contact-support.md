# Contacting customer support

## Live chat

Live chat is open **9am to 6pm, Monday to Friday** (UK time) from the help icon on every page.

## Email

Email **support@shopwave.example**. We reply within **1 working day**.

## Phone

ShopWave does **not** have a customer phone line. Chat and email are the fastest ways to reach us.

## Changing or cancelling an order

You can change the address or cancel an order within **1 hour** of placing it, from the "My orders" page. After that the order is already being packed; you can return it once it arrives.
