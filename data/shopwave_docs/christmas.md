# Christmas delivery and returns

## Last order dates for delivery before Christmas

| Service | Order by |
|---|---|
| UK standard | 18 December |
| UK express | 21 December, 2pm |
| European Union | 10 December |
| Rest of the world | 3 December |

## Extended Christmas returns

Items bought between **1 November and 24 December** can be returned until **31 January**, whatever the usual 30-day limit says. Sale items and personalised items keep their normal rules.
