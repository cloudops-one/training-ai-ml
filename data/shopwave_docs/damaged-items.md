# Damaged, faulty or wrong items

Glass and ceramics sometimes break in transit. We are sorry when this happens and we make it quick to fix.

## Report it within 7 days

Contact support within **7 days of delivery** with:

- your order number,
- a photo of the damaged item and of the box it came in.

## What we do

- You choose a **replacement** (if in stock) or a **full refund**, including the delivery charge.
- **Items worth under £15: keep them or recycle them.** You do not need to send them back.
- Items worth £15 or more: we email you a free returns label, UK or international.

## Wrong item sent

If we sent the wrong item, we send the right one by express delivery at no cost and include a returns label for the wrong one.
