# International shipping

ShopWave ships to 38 countries.

## European Union

- Delivery takes 5 to 8 working days and costs £9.95. It is free on orders over £100.
- For EU orders under €150 we collect VAT at checkout, so there is nothing extra to pay on arrival.
- For EU orders of €150 or more, the courier may charge import VAT and a handling fee on delivery. These charges are paid by the customer.

## Rest of the world

- Delivery takes 7 to 14 working days and costs £14.95. There is no free-delivery threshold.
- Customs duties and import taxes are the customer's responsibility.

## Restrictions

- We cannot deliver to PO boxes outside the UK.
- We do not currently ship to Russia or Belarus.
- Candles and reed diffusers cannot be sent by air to Australia or New Zealand.
