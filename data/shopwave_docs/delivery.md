# Delivery within the UK

ShopWave ships every UK order from our warehouse in Leicester.

## Options and prices

- **Standard delivery** takes 3 to 5 working days and costs £3.95. It is free on orders over £40.
- **Express delivery** arrives the next working day if you order before 2pm (Monday to Friday). It costs £7.95 and is never free.
- We do not deliver on Sundays or bank holidays. An order placed on a Friday after 2pm with express delivery arrives on Tuesday.

## Tracking

As soon as your parcel leaves the warehouse we email you a tracking link. If the link shows no movement for 3 working days, contact support and we will chase the courier.

## Missed deliveries

If nobody is home, the courier leaves a card and tries again the next working day. After two failed attempts the parcel goes to your nearest collection point for 7 days, then comes back to us and we refund the order minus the delivery charge.
