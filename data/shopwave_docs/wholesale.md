# Wholesale and trade accounts

Shops, cafés and other businesses can buy from ShopWave at trade prices.

## Opening a trade account

- Apply on the "Trade" page with your company name and **VAT number** (or company registration number outside the UK).
- We review applications within **2 working days**.

## Prices and minimums

- Trade prices are about **25% below** our retail prices.
- The **first order must be at least £150**. After that the minimum is **£100**.
- Most products are sold in packs of 6, 12 or 24.

## Payment and delivery

- New trade accounts pay at checkout by card.
- After **three paid orders** you can apply for **30-day invoice terms**.
- Wholesale orders ship within **3 working days**. Delivery to UK businesses is free over £300.
